AFTER FLUTTERWAVE CHECKOUT

Check your payment and ticket email

If you just completed payment on Flutterwave, check your email for Flutterwave's receipt and any ticket delivery details.

What happens next?

  1. Check your inbox and spam folder for a Flutterwave payment receipt.
  2. Ticket delivery is handled by the event organiser; this static website cannot verify payment or issue tickets.
  3. If you were charged but have no ticket information, contact OnyXLuxe support with your Flutterwave transaction receipt.

This page is informational only. It does not confirm whether a payment succeeded.
